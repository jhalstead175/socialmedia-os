# SoshlOps FAQ

---

## Q1: What is SoshlOps?

SoshlOps is a platform for managing social media as an operational function. It provides tools for publishing, scheduling, visibility, and oversight across platforms.

---

## Q2: Who is SoshlOps designed for?

SoshlOps is designed for agencies, corporate teams, and professionals who require structured workflows and controlled access.

---

## Q3: Is SoshlOps built for creators or influencers?

No. SoshlOps is not designed for creator workflows or growth optimization. It prioritizes operational clarity over content experimentation.

---

## Q4: Does SoshlOps automatically publish content?

No. SoshlOps does not perform autonomous publishing. All actions are intentional and user-initiated.

---

## Q5: Does SoshlOps use AI?

SoshlOps offers optional, assistive drafting support. It does not generate, publish, or manage content autonomously.

---

## Q6: Which platforms are supported?

SoshlOps supports X (Twitter), LinkedIn, and Meta platforms. Additional platforms are not supported in v1.

---

## Q7: Does SoshlOps provide real-time analytics?

No. SoshlOps provides summary-level visibility intended for review. It does not provide real-time monitoring or predictive analysis.

---

## Q8: Can teams collaborate inside SoshlOps?

No. Team collaboration and role-based access are not included in v1.

---

## Q9: Is SoshlOps compliant with specific regulations?

SoshlOps does not claim compliance with any specific regulatory framework. It is designed to support professional operational use.

---

## Q10: Is SoshlOps suitable for regulated industries?

Yes. SoshlOps is designed with restrained scope and neutral controls that may be appropriate for regulated environments. Organizations should perform their own evaluations.

---

## Q11: Does SoshlOps store or analyze private messages?

SoshlOps displays message summaries for visibility. It does not analyze, automate, or act on message content.

---

## Q12: How does pricing work?

Pricing is based on operational scale. Plans reflect limits on connected accounts and usage.

---

## Q13: Is SoshlOps in active development?

SoshlOps is intentionally focused. Changes are evaluated carefully to preserve operational clarity.
